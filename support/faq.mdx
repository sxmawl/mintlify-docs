## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Cardboard?">
    Cardboard is an AI video editor. Describe an edit, review the result, and refine it in chat or on a timeline. You can use the web editor, the Apple Silicon Mac app, or the Cardboard connector in Claude and ChatGPT.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to download anything?">
    The [web editor](https://www.cardboard.ai/app/home) runs in your browser. The [Mac app](https://www.cardboard.ai/desktop) is a separate download and includes the `cb` command-line tool. To use Cardboard in Claude or ChatGPT, follow the [connector setup guide](https://docs.cardboard.ai/mcp).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which browsers and devices can I use?">
    Use Chrome on a computer for the web editor. Microsoft Edge is also supported. The browser needs WebGL2; Safari, Firefox, Arc, and Brave are currently blocked by the editor's compatibility check.

    The full editor needs a screen at least 1024 pixels wide. On a phone, use the [Claude or ChatGPT connector](https://docs.cardboard.ai/mcp).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does Cardboard handle my data?">
    Read the [Privacy Policy](https://www.cardboard.ai/privacy) for how Cardboard collects, uses, protects, and retains data. The connector also has [data-handling documentation](https://docs.cardboard.ai/mcp/data).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Editing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can I ask the AI to do?">
    Ask it to find footage, trim and arrange clips, create highlights, add captions, adjust pacing, or add music, transitions, and effects. Web image and video generation is available on Starter and above, with premium models on Creator and Pro. See [How to Talk to Cardboard](/learn/talk).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I undo an AI edit?">
    Ask in chat, for example “Undo that,” or describe the correction you want. Use **Cmd/Ctrl+Z** to undo your own timeline edits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which languages can I caption?">
    Transcription supports 30 languages. Auto-detect works for many of them; choose a language explicitly if it is not detected correctly. Review names, spelling, and timing after generation, especially with background noise or overlapping speakers. See [Captions](/learn/captions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Media and projects

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which file formats can I upload?">
    The web editor accepts video in MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, and MKV; audio in MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, and M4A; and images in JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, and HEIC/HEIF. Whether a file decodes successfully also depends on its codec. The Mac app supports additional formats.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the upload size limit?">
    Web uploads allow up to **2.5 GB per file** on Lite, Starter, and Creator, or **10 GB per file** on Pro. These are not local Mac import limits. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many projects can I have?">
    Current Lite, Starter, Creator, and Pro plans have no project limit. Older subscriptions can have different limits, including a legacy Creator limit of 5 existing projects in total, not per month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do my projects sync between the web, Mac app, and connector?">
    No. Web projects belong to your web account, Mac projects are local bundles, and connector projects are separate. See [Manage Your Account](/account/manage-your-account).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can other people review my video?">
    In the web editor, create a review link for your project. Anyone with the link can watch and leave timecoded comments. This is not simultaneous editing or a shared team workspace. For team needs, [contact Support](https://www.cardboard.ai/support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Exports

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which video formats and resolutions are available?">
    Video export supports MP4, WebM, MOV, and MKV. Lite and Starter export up to 1080p; Creator and Pro export up to 4K. Available codec choices depend on the export environment. See [Formats & Quality](/export/formats-and-quality).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I continue editing in Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve?">
    On Starter and above, the web editor can export FCPXML for Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve, and XML for Premiere Pro. Timeline export is not available in the Mac app. See [Export Your Video](/export/export-your-video).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many exports are included, and is there a watermark?">
    Lite includes **3 lifetime exports**, up to 1080p, with a Cardboard watermark. This applies to paid web Lite too. Starter, Creator, and Pro have unlimited watermark-free video exports.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where does rendering happen, and how long does it take?">
    Web exports can render in the browser or cloud. The Mac app renders locally; the Claude/ChatGPT connector renders in the cloud. Time depends on the project, resolution, effects, device, and rendering path. See [Export Your Video](/export/export-your-video).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    There is no free trial on the web. Lite is free in the [Mac app](https://www.cardboard.ai/desktop); all web plans are paid. See [current pricing](https://www.cardboard.ai/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription?">
    Yes. Open **Settings → Billing** in the web app. Cancel any time; your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period and then does not renew.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which payment methods are accepted?">
    Use a payment method shown at checkout. See [pricing](https://www.cardboard.ai/pricing) for current monthly and annual options.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
