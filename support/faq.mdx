## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Cardboard?">
    Cardboard is a browser-based AI-powered video editor. You can upload video footage, edit it using plain-English AI commands or manual timeline editing, and export professional-quality videos — all from your browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to download anything?">
    No. Cardboard runs entirely in your browser. There is nothing to install. You just need a modern browser like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cardboard work on mobile?">
    Cardboard is optimized for desktop and laptop browsers. You can access it on tablets, but the full editing experience works best on a screen that is at least 13 inches wide.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my content secure?">
    Yes. Your media files are encrypted in transit and at rest. Cardboard does not use your content to train AI models. Only you and people you share a project with can access your files.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What browsers are supported?">
    Cardboard works best on the latest versions of Chrome and Edge. Safari and Firefox are also supported. We recommend Chrome for the best performance.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI editing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does AI editing work?">
    You type a plain-English instruction in the **AI Chat** panel (for example, "trim to the best 30 seconds" or "add captions"). The AI analyzes your video, decides what to do, and applies the edits to your timeline. You can see exactly what changed and undo anything. Learn more in [How to Talk to Cardboard](/learn/talk).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can the AI do?">
    The AI can trim, split, and rearrange clips. It can create montages, add transitions, generate captions, adjust pacing, add music, apply effects, find specific moments in your footage, and much more. See [How to Talk to Cardboard](/learn/talk) for examples.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I undo AI edits?">
    Yes. Every AI edit can be undone with **Cmd/Ctrl + Z** or by typing "Undo that" in the AI Chat. Your original media files are never modified.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the AI work in languages other than English?">
    The AI Chat currently accepts instructions in English. However, the AI can transcribe and caption videos in 20+ languages. See [Auto-Generated Captions](/learn/captions) for the full list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How accurate are AI-generated captions?">
    Captions are highly accurate for clear audio in supported languages. You can always review and edit captions after they are generated. Background noise or overlapping speakers may reduce accuracy.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Media & projects

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What file formats can I upload?">
    Cardboard supports:
    - **Video** — MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV
    - **Audio** — MP3, WAV, AAC
    - **Images** — PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a file size limit?">
    File size limits depend on your plan — up to 1 GB on Creator and up to 5 GB on Pro. Total storage also varies by plan. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many projects can I have?">
    The number of projects depends on your plan — Creator allows 5 per month, while Pro and Teams are unlimited. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I collaborate with others on a project?">
    Team collaboration is available on the Teams plan. You can invite team members, share projects, and work together. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Exporting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What export formats are available?">
    You can export as MP4, WebM, MOV, or MKV. Resolutions range from 480p to 4K. See [Formats & Quality Settings](/export/formats-and-quality) for recommendations by platform.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export to Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve?">
    Yes. Cardboard can export an XML timeline that you can import into Adobe Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve for further editing. See [Export Your Video](/export/export-your-video) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does exporting take?">
    Export time depends on the video length, resolution, and complexity. A typical 2-minute 1080p video takes 30 seconds to a few minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a watermark on exported videos?">
    There is no watermark on any paid plan. Free trial exports may include a small Cardboard watermark.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. You can try Cardboard free for 7 days with full access to all features. No credit card required to start. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription?">
    Yes. You can cancel anytime from your [account settings](/account/manage-your-account). Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Cardboard accepts all major credit and debit cards. Annual plans are also available at a discounted rate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
