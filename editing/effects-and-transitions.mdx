Use the editor's left rail to open **Transitions** or **Effects**. You can also ask AI Chat for a specific change, then preview the result.

## Transitions

Drag a transition from the panel onto the cut between two clips. The picker includes Fade, Dissolve, Fly, Push, Slide, Zoom, Light Leak, Film Burn, Glitch, Mask Glitch, Chromatic Aberration, Ink Bleed, and Radial Wipe.

Adjust duration and easing. Direction controls are available for directional transitions such as Fly, Push, and Slide. Mask Glitch uses a subject cutout; follow the setup shown for that transition.

Audio fades are separate from visual transitions. See [Music & Audio](/editing/music-and-audio).

## Color looks

Open **Effects → Color looks**. Available looks include Cinematic, Black & White, Vintage, Cool, Warm, High Contrast, Faded Film, Technicolor, and Newsreel.

Color looks use adjustment layers above your footage. Adjust the layer's duration to control which part of the video it affects, and use the intensity control from **0% to 100%** to adjust its strength.

## Keyframe animation

Keyframes set a property's value at particular times. Cardboard interpolates between those values to create movement or a gradual change.

1. Select a video, image, or graphic clip and move the playhead to the start of the change.
2. Use the property's diamond control to add a keyframe and set the value.
3. Move the playhead to another time, add another keyframe, and set the next value.
4. Preview the result and adjust the timing or values.

You can animate position, scale, rotation, and opacity. Video and image clips also support blur and corner-radius keyframes. Press **U** with the default shortcuts to show or hide keyframe lanes.

## Example requests

- “Add a dissolve between the interview and the B-roll.”
- “Apply a warm color look to this section.”
- “Zoom in slowly on the subject over 2 seconds.”
