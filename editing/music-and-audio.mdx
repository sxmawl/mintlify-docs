In the web editor, open **Audio** in the left rail to browse music and sound effects. You can also import your own audio or ask the agent to choose from the built-in library.

## Music and sound effects

Search the music library and filter by mood. Preview a track, then add it to your timeline and trim it to the section you want.

Sound effects are grouped into **Input, Vintage & Film, Impact & Bass, UI & Alerts, Transition, and Foley**. Preview an effect and position it at the moment it should play.

For licensing information, see Cardboard's [Terms](https://www.cardboard.ai/terms). Make sure you have the necessary rights to audio you import yourself.

## Volume and fades

Select an audio or video clip to adjust its audio properties. Clip volume ranges from **0% to 200%**: 0% is silent and 100% is the original level.

Use fade-in and fade-out controls to ease into or out of a clip. Adjust their duration and easing, then listen to the transition.

Listen to dialogue and music together. Lower the music if it masks speech; do not rely on a fixed volume percentage being right for every recording.

## Clean up dialogue

The clip's right-click menu includes **Clean voice**, **Remove background music**, and **Split vocals and instrumental**. These cloud operations use credits and work on web projects. Preview the result before using it in the final edit.

## Cut to a beat

The agent can analyze a music track and use beat timing to arrange clips. Ask, for example:

> Sync the cuts to the beat of @music.mp3, and keep the final shot longer.

Beat analysis is used by the agent; it does not add editable beat markers to the timeline. Preview and refine the cuts after the edit.

## Example audio requests

- “Add quiet background music under the narration.”
- “Lower the music during the speaking parts.”
- “Add a click sound when the button appears.”
- “Fade out the music over the last 3 seconds.”
