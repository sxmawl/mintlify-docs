Open **Library** in the left rail to browse your project media, preview it, and drag clips onto the timeline.

## Import media

Add files from your computer, or use **Cmd/Ctrl+I** with the default shortcuts. The web editor accepts:

| Media | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Video | MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, MKV |
| Audio | MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, M4A |
| Images | JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC/HEIF |

Decoding also depends on the codec inside the file. The Mac app accepts additional formats.

Web uploads allow up to 2.5 GB per file on Lite, Starter, and Creator, or 10 GB on Pro. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing).

Cardboard processes media in the background for previews, transcription, and search. You can begin arranging clips while processing continues; search results depend on the analysis that has finished.

## Search project media

Use **Search project media…** in the Library. Search modes include **All**, **Visual**, and **Speech**. Search can use visual descriptions, speech transcripts, on-screen text, and summaries.

Try queries such as:

- “Person holding a phone”
- “City skyline”
- “Mentions the product launch”
- “Introduces the team”

You can also ask in chat:

> Find shots where someone is talking to camera.

## Find music and sound effects

For library music and sound effects, use the **Audio** panel or ask the agent. This searches the built-in catalog; project-media search does not identify every ambient sound or music genre in your uploaded clips.
