Cardboard can transcribe speech and turn it into captions. Review the text and timing before exporting, especially for names, background noise, and overlapping speakers.

## Generate captions

Ask in the AI Chat panel on the right:

> Add captions to the video.

You can also open **Captions** in the left rail to use the caption controls. Allow transcription to finish, then preview the result.

## Edit the text and timing

- Double-click a caption in the video preview, or edit its text in the **Transcript** tab.
- Adjust a caption's timing by dragging its edges on the timeline.
- Select a caption and press Backspace to delete it with the default shortcuts.
- Ask in chat for changes, such as “Break long captions into shorter lines.”

## Style captions

Choose a caption preset, then use the floating caption toolbar to adjust the available styling controls, including font, size, color, and background. Position the caption in the preview.

Each preset has its own animation. Choose another preset to change the animation. Use **Apply to all** in the caption toolbar to apply the style across your captions.

## Languages

Transcription supports 30 languages. **Auto** is the default, but it cannot detect every supported language. Choose the language explicitly if detection is incorrect or your language is not supported by Auto.
