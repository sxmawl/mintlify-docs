Use the **Voice** tab in the web editor's left rail to create narration from text. These instructions cover the web editor; the Mac app does not have this Voice panel.

## Generate a voiceover

1. Open **Voice** and type or paste your script, up to **5,000 characters** in the panel.
2. Choose a preset voice or browse the voice library. Preview the voice before generating.
3. Adjust the voice settings if needed.
4. Click **Generate** and listen to the result.
5. Click **Add to Timeline** to place it at the playhead.

Generating requires an available credit balance. Creator and Pro also support voice cloning. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing) for current plan information.

## Voice settings

- **Stability:** Adjust consistency and variation in delivery.
- **Similarity:** Adjust how closely the output follows the selected voice.
- **Style:** Adjust stylistic intensity.
- **Speed:** Choose from **0.7× to 1.2×** in the panel.
- **Speaker boost:** Enable the available voice enhancement option.

Listen after changing settings; the same script can sound different across voices.

## Edit the narration

Once added, move and trim the voiceover like an audio clip. Adjust its volume and fades in the clip properties. Generate another take if you want different wording or delivery.

You can also ask in web AI Chat:

> Add a voiceover that says: Welcome to our product tour.

To add text on screen, ask for [captions](/learn/captions), then review their spelling and timing.
