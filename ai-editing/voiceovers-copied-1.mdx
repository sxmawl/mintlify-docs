## What You Need

- 10-15 video clips (action shots, different angles, variety)
- A song with a clear beat (upbeat, energetic)

## How to Make It

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Everything">
    Drag all your clips into Cardboard. Add your music track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell AI What You Want">
    **"Create a 45-second beat sync montage using all clips. Sync to @roadtrip-music.mp3, make it vertical for Instagram, add 'Road Trip 2025' title at the start"**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fine-Tune">
    > "Make cuts even tighter—1 second per clip max"

    > "Keep the sunset clip longer, it's the best one"

    > "Add 'Summer 2025' title at the start"

    > "Make it vertical for TikTok"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Do manual tweaking if needed and then Export
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Commom Prompts

Fine-tune how the video  with prompts like:

> "Sync all cuts to the music beat"

> "Time the scene change to the beat drop at 0:08"

> "Add slow-motion on every fourth beat"

> "Make cuts faster during the chorus, slower during verses"

## Quick tips

- **Pick the right music:** Clear beat = easier sync. EDM, hip-hop, pop works great.
- **Use varied shots:** Wide, close-ups, different angles
- **First 3 seconds:** Your best shot. Hook them immediately.
- **Every beat:** A new clip or movement.

<Tip>
  You can also use the AI Chat panel to generate voiceovers — try "Add a voiceover that says: Welcome to our product tour" or "Generate a calm female voiceover reading this script."
</Tip>
