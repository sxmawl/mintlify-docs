Start with a podcast recording and choose one idea for a short clip. Each project has one timeline; use a separate project for each independently editable and exportable highlight.

## Make a highlight

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the recording">
    Add your podcast video or audio and any logo or speaker graphics. Check the [web upload limit](/account/plans-and-pricing); long recordings can also take longer to process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find one strong moment">
    Ask the agent:

    > Find the strongest 45-second moment about starting a business. Make it a standalone vertical clip, keeping enough context to understand the speaker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Polish the clip">
    Review the selection, then ask for changes:

    - “Add captions and check the speaker's name.”
    - “Remove long pauses without changing the meaning.”
    - “Add a title with the main takeaway.”
    - “Add the podcast logo in the corner.”
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and export">
    Watch the whole clip, check captions and audio, then [export](/export/export-your-video). Repeat in a separate project for another highlight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other prompts

- “Pull the section about pricing and make it understandable on its own.”
- “Make a one-minute compilation of the funniest moments.”
- “Start with the main insight, then include the explanation.”

Keep one clear idea per clip. Avoid removing context that changes what the speaker meant.
