## What You Need

- Talking head clips (you on camera)
- B-roll (what you're doing, places you go)
- Optional: Voiceover or music

## How to Make It

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Everything">
    All your clips go into Cardboard. Don't organize. Just upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell AI What You Want">
    "Create a 4-minute vlog. Start with coffee (hook), then morning routine, coffee shop, gym, dinner. Add captions to talking, B-roll during voiceover, background music. 16:9 horizontal."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fine-Tune">
    "Add captions to all talking parts"

    "Remove all pauses longer than 2 seconds"

    "Add a title card: 'A Day in My Life'"

    "Lower the music volume when I'm talking"

    "Make it warmer and more golden hour vibes"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Do manual tweaking if needed and then Export
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Prompts

Fine-tune your video with prompts like:

> "Match B-roll to my voiceover timing"
>
> "Remove all the 'um's and pauses"
>
> "Add smooth transitions between morning, afternoon, evening"
>
> "Speed up boring parts, keep good moments normal speed"
>
> "Add my face in a small window during screen recordings"

## Vlog Structure

**Hook (0-5 sec):** Your best moment or tease

**Intro (5-20 sec):** "Hey guys, today I'm..."

**Body (20 sec - 4 min):** Your day, mix talking + B-roll

**Outro (last 30 sec):** Wrap up, CTA
