## What You Need

- Screen recordings (if explaining software)
- Footage or stock clips (if explaining a concept)
- Voiceover script
- Optional: Graphics or diagrams

## How to Make It

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write Your Script">
    Keep it simple:

    - What's the problem?
    - What's the solution?
    - How does it work?
    - Why does it matter?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Voiceover">
    Record yourself or use AI:

    "Generate a voiceover: [paste script]"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Your Footage">
    Screen recordings, B-roll, graphics, everything goes in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell AI to build">
    "Build an explainer video around @voiceover.m4a. Match visuals to what I'm saying. Use screen recordings for 'how it works,' B-roll for problem/benefit sections. Add captions. 90 seconds."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Clarity">
    "Add text overlay: 'Step 1: Sign Up' at 0:15, 'Step 2: Connect' at 0:30"

    "Zoom in on the login button at 0:20"

    "Add an arrow pointing to the settings icon"

    "Add captions to the entire voiceover"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Do manual tweaking if needed and then Export
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Commom Prompts

Fine-tune how the video  with prompts like:

> "Create a 60-second explainer showing how [product] works"
>
> "Match screen recordings to my voiceover"
>
> "Add step numbers (1, 2, 3) as text overlays"
>
> "Zoom in on important UI elements"
>
> "Add arrows highlighting key features"

## Video structure

**Hook (0-5 sec):** State the problem

**Solution (5-20 sec):** Introduce your product/concept

**How It Works (20-75 sec):** 3-5 steps max

**Benefits (75-85 sec):** Why this matters

**CTA (85-90 sec):** "Try it free"

## Quick Tips

**Keep it simple.** One concept per video.

**Show, don't tell.** Screen recordings \> descriptions.

**Pace matters.** Give people time to absorb.

**Captions are mandatory.** People watch on mute.
