## How it works

Think of AI Chat as your editing assistant who never gets tired. You tell it what you want, and it makes it happen.

Here's what I need for my vlog reel:

- Tighter pacing (no dead air)
- Captions so people can watch without sound
- Background music to set the vibe
- Maybe a title card to hook viewers

Let's do it.

## What the AI can do

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Making Your Video Snappier">
    My first draft has some slow moments. Let's fix that.

    > Type this in AI Chat (right panel): **"Remove all silent parts and tighten the pacing"**

    Hit Enter. AI scans your video, finds the pauses, and cuts them out. Your video now flows better.

    Watch it. Too fast?

    > Tell AI: **"Keep a 0.5-second pause between clips for breathing room"**

    AI adjusts. You're in control.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Adding Captions">
    80% of people scroll without sound. Captions are non-negotiable.

    > Type: **"Add captions to the whole video"**

    AI transcribes your voiceover and adds captions. They appear on the timeline.

    Want to tweak the style?

    >  Try: **"Make captions bold and yellow" **or 
    >
    > **"Put captions at the top of the screen"**

    AI updates them instantly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Dropping in Background Music">
    Every good video has a vibe. Music sets it.

    > Type: **"Add upbeat background music"**

    AI picks a track that matches your video's energy and adds it to the timeline.

    Don't like the vibe? 

    > Be specific:**"Add chill lo-fi background music" or** 
    >
    >  **"Add energetic electronic music"**

    AI swaps it out. You can also tell it to adjust the volume: 

    > **"Lower the background music volume so the voiceover is clear"**
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Fine-Tuning the Details">
    Here's where you make it _yours_. Try these:

    **Speed up slow sections:**

    > "Speed up the middle section to 1.5x"

    **Make it platform-ready:**

    > "Make this 15 seconds for TikTok"

    **Add transitions:**

    > "Add smooth transitions between clips"

    **Highlight a moment:**

    > "Zoom in on the coffee cup at 0:12"

    **Colour grade it:**

    > "Add a warm, cinematic color grade"
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Each prompt, AI adjusts. You watch, you iterate, you perfect.

## Tips for better prompts

- **Iterate.** Start with a big edit, then refine with follow-up commands.
- **Be specific:** "Add captions" works. "Add bold white captions at the bottom with a black background" works better.
- **Reference timestamps:** "Zoom in at 0:15" or "Speed up from 0:10 to 0:25"
- **Use the @ feature:** "Match cuts to the beat of @background-music.mp3"
- **Stack requests:** "Add captions, remove silent parts, and add upbeat music" (AI handles all three)

<Tip>
  The AI remembers your conversation within a project. You can say things like "Actually, undo that and make it 45 seconds instead."
</Tip>

## **When AI Doesn't Get It Right**

Sometimes AI misunderstands. No worries.

- **Hit undo** – Use Command+Z on Mac or Ctrl+Z on Windows
- **Clarify your prompt** – "Actually, make the captions smaller and move them to the top"
- **Try a different approach** – Instead of "make it better," say "add transitions and speed it up"

AI learns from your feedback. The more specific you are, the better it gets.

<Warning>
  If you undo an AI edit and then make a new edit, the undone changes are lost. This is the same as undo/redo in any editor.
</Warning>

## **You're Ready**

You've got captions, music, smooth pacing, and a hook. Your video reel is ready to post.

Hit **Export** (top-right), pick your settings, and download it.

Or keep tweaking. There's no limit to iterations. Make it exactly what you want.
