This page is a reference guide of ready-to-use prompts for Cardboard's AI Chat. Copy any prompt below, paste it into the **AI Chat** panel, and hit **Enter**.

<Tip>
  These prompts work as written, but you can customize them. Change durations, styles, or details to match your project.
</Tip>

## Social media clips

Great for turning longer content into short-form videos for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and more.

- "Turn this into a 15-second Instagram Reel"
- "Make a vertical 9:16 clip for TikTok"
- "Find the most engaging 60 seconds for a YouTube Short"
- "Create 3 different 30-second clips from this video"
- "Add captions, make it vertical, and keep it under 60 seconds"
- "Add a hook in the first 3 seconds to grab attention"
- "End with a call-to-action that says 'Follow for more'"

## Presentations & talking heads

For cleaning up recorded presentations, webinars, lectures, and talking-head videos.

- "Remove all the ums, ahs, and long pauses"
- "Cut out the parts where the speaker is looking at notes"
- "Speed up the slow sections to keep the pace up"
- "Add lower-third titles when each new speaker starts"
- "Create a highlight reel of the key points"
- "Trim the first 2 minutes — that's just the intro"
- "Add captions and make the text large enough for mobile"

## Vlogs & personal videos

For editing travel vlogs, day-in-the-life videos, and personal content.

- "Create a cinematic travel montage with smooth transitions"
- "Add trending background music that matches the vibe"
- "Make a 60-second recap of this entire vlog"
- "Add text overlays showing the location name"
- "Speed up the walking and driving clips to 4x"
- "Add a vintage film look to the whole video"
- "Create a timelapse effect for the sunset shots"

## Marketing & product videos

For promotional videos, product demos, ads, and brand content.

- "Make a 30-second product demo with a clean, modern feel"
- "Add text highlighting the 3 key features"
- "Create an attention-grabbing intro in the first 2 seconds"
- "Add the company logo at the beginning and end"
- "Make this look professional with smooth transitions"
- "Add background music that feels upbeat and trustworthy"
- "Create a before-and-after comparison"

## Podcasts & interviews

For editing recorded podcasts, interviews, and conversations.

- "Remove all dead air and long pauses"
- "Create a 2-minute highlight clip of the best moments"
- "Add captions so people can watch without sound"
- "Show the speaker's name when they first start talking"
- "Find the part where they talk about [topic] and make it a clip"
- "Add an intro bumper and outro to the recording"
- "Balance the audio so both speakers are the same volume"

## Quick fixes

Common one-line commands for fast edits.

- "Add a fade-in at the start and fade-out at the end"
- "Stabilize shaky footage"
- "Brighten the dark clips"
- "Crop to 16:9"
- "Reverse this clip"
- "Loop this clip 3 times"
- "Mute the original audio"
- "Add 2 seconds of black at the beginning"
- "Slow down to half speed"
- "Mirror the video horizontally"

## Combining prompts

You can chain multiple instructions in a single prompt for complex edits:

- "Make it vertical for TikTok, add captions, remove silent parts, and add upbeat music"
- "Trim to the best 45 seconds, add a title card, and export at 1080p"
- "Find the funniest moments, create a montage, add transitions, and speed up to keep it under 30 seconds"
