---
title: "Your First Video"
description: "Add footage, ask for an edit, refine it, and export in the web editor."
---

Open [Cardboard](https://www.cardboard.ai/app/home) in Chrome on a computer. For editing inside Claude or ChatGPT, use the [connector quickstart](https://docs.cardboard.ai/mcp).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your footage">
    Add the video, audio, and images you want to use. You can choose a starter such as Vlog, Montage, or Product demo to begin your prompt, then add your own footage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your video">
    Give Cardboard a goal and a few concrete constraints:

    > Make a 45-second vertical vlog from these clips. Start with the coffee shop, then show the walk through the city. Add captions to the speaking parts and quiet background music.

    Use **@** to mention a particular file. Choose the canvas size next to Send, then send your prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the first cut">
    Watch the result and check the order, timing, captions, and audio.

    - **Left:** Library and tools for layout, audio, captions, voice, text, transitions, and effects. Selecting a clip opens its properties here.
    - **Centre:** The video preview.
    - **Bottom:** The timeline, where you can trim, split, and move clips.
    - **Right:** AI Chat, where you can ask for changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine the edit">
    Try “Shorten the opening by 3 seconds,” “Make the captions smaller,” or “Lower the music under the dialogue.” You can also adjust clips directly on the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export** at the top right. Choose a format, resolution, and quality, then start the export and save the file.

    Lite includes 3 lifetime exports with a watermark, up to 1080p. Starter has unlimited watermark-free exports up to 1080p; Creator and Pro support up to 4K. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing).
  </Step>
</Steps>
