## What's included in every plan

Every Cardboard plan includes:

- Full AI-powered video editor
- AI Chat for plain-English editing
- Auto-generated captions
- AI voiceovers
- Built-in music and sound effects library
- All transitions and color presets
- Export to MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV
- Export to Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, and DaVinci Resolve

## Billing

Plans are available on **monthly** or **annual** billing. Annual plans offer a discount compared to monthly.

## Changing your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from **Account Settings**:

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner of the editor
2. Select **Account Settings**
3. Go to the **Subscription** section
4. Choose your new plan

Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
