Cardboard offers **Lite, Starter, Creator, and Pro**. See the [current pricing page](https://www.cardboard.ai/pricing) for prices, billing options, included usage, and offers.

## Free access and trials

Lite is free in the [Mac app](https://www.cardboard.ai/desktop). All web plans, including Lite, are paid. There is no free trial on the web.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Lite | Starter | Creator | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Video exports | 3 lifetime exports | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Watermark | Yes, including paid web Lite | No | No | No |
| Maximum export resolution | 1080p | 1080p | 4K | 4K |
| Maximum web upload per file | 2.5 GB | 2.5 GB | 2.5 GB | 10 GB |
| Project limit on current plans | None | None | None | None |
| Timeline export to other editors, on the web | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Image and video generation models | Not included | Base models | Base and premium models | Base and premium models |

Lite's export allowance is a lifetime total, not a monthly allowance. It also includes limited agent time. The Export dialog shows how many Lite exports you have left.

The upload limits above apply to web uploads, not to local files imported into the Mac app. Timeline export to Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and Premiere Pro is available in the web editor on Starter and above; it is not available in the Mac app.

Older subscriptions may retain different limits. For example, the legacy Creator plan has a limit of 5 existing projects in total, not 5 new projects each month. Check **Settings → Billing** for your subscription.

## Credits and usage

AI editing and cloud features have usage allowances and may consume credits. Generation also depends on your plan's model access. See [pricing](https://www.cardboard.ai/pricing) for included usage and **Settings → Usage** for your balance. If you reach a limit, check your renewal date or available upgrades.

## Sharing and team needs

The web editor supports review links so other people can watch and leave timecoded comments. This is different from a shared workspace or simultaneous editing.

Teams is not a standard self-serve tier in the current plan catalog. For team or high-volume needs, [contact Cardboard](https://www.cardboard.ai/support).

## Change or cancel a plan

In the web app, open your avatar menu, choose **Settings**, then **Billing**.

- Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at your next billing date.
- Cancel any time; your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period and then does not renew.
- Payment methods and amounts due are shown before you confirm.

See [Manage Your Account](/account/manage-your-account) for profile, billing, and account settings.
