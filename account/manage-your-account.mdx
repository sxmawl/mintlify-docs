## Open settings

In the web app, click your avatar and choose **Settings**.

| Section | What you can manage |
| --- | --- |
| Profile | Name, email address, and profile photo |
| Account | Active sessions, Log out everywhere, and Delete account |
| Notifications | Email preferences |
| Billing | Subscription, payment method, invoices, and cancellation |
| Usage | Usage and credit balances |

To change plans, open **Billing**. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades apply at your next billing date. Cancelling a paid subscription stops its renewal at the end of the current billing period.

The Mac app has its own Settings sections, including Library, Agents, Account, Usage, Shortcuts, and Privacy. For keyboard shortcuts on the web, use the Cardboard brand menu; on Mac, use **Settings → Shortcuts**.

## Where your projects live

- **Web:** Projects are saved to your account. Sign in to the same account in a supported browser on another computer to open them. Allow uploads and sync to finish before switching computers.
- **Mac app:** Projects are saved locally as `.cardboard` bundles.
- **Claude and ChatGPT:** Connector projects are separate from web and Mac projects. See [connector permissions](https://docs.cardboard.ai/mcp/permissions).

These project libraries do not automatically sync with one another.

## Delete your account

In the web app, go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**. If a subscription is active, cancel it under **Billing** first, as prompted in the account screen.

For information about personal data and data requests, read the [Privacy Policy](https://www.cardboard.ai/privacy) or [contact Support](https://www.cardboard.ai/support).
