Your Cardboard account lets you manage your profile, subscription, and preferences all in one place.

## Accessing account settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your avatar">
    Click your avatar or profile icon in the top-right corner of the editor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Account Settings">
    Choose **Account Settings** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can manage

### Profile

- **Name** — Your display name
- **Email** — The email address associated with your account
- **Avatar** — Your profile picture

### Security

- **Password** — Change your account password
- **Sessions and devices** — View and manage active sessions across your devices

### Subscription

- **Current plan** — See which plan you are on (Creator, Pro, or Teams)
- **Upgrade or downgrade** — Switch plans at any time
- **Billing history** — View past invoices and payment details

### Preferences

- **Voice settings** — Your preferred voiceover voice and settings are synced across sessions
- **Editor preferences** — Settings like snap behavior and default export quality

## Cloud projects

When you are signed in, your projects are automatically backed up to the cloud. This means you can access them from any device by logging into your account.

<Tip>
  Your projects are local-first — they are always available on your device even if you are offline. Cloud backup happens automatically when you are connected.
</Tip>

## Deleting your account

If you need to delete your account, email [founders@usecardboard.com](mailto:founders@usecardboard.com) and the team will process your request. Account deletion removes your profile, subscription, and cloud-stored projects.
