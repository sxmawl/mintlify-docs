title: "Formats & Quality Settings"
description: "Choose video formats, codecs, aspect ratios, resolution, and frame rate."
---

Format, codec, resolution, frame rate, and quality are in **Export → Video File**. Available choices depend on your plan and export environment.

## Video formats

| Format | Video codecs | Audio codec |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MP4 | H.264, HEVC (H.265), AV1 | AAC |
| MOV | H.264, HEVC (H.265) | AAC |
| WebM | VP9, AV1 | Opus |
| MKV | H.264, HEVC (H.265), AV1, VP9 | AAC |

The browser filters codec choices to supported encoders. For broad playback compatibility, start with MP4 and H.264. Check the receiving platform's requirements before delivery.

## Aspect ratios and resolution

Set the canvas to **16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, or 4:5** from the aspect-ratio picker in the editor's top bar, or next to Send on Home. Choose the aspect ratio for your intended layout: landscape, portrait, or square.

| Resolution label | 16:9 | 9:16 | 1:1 | 4:3 | 3:4 | 4:5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 480p | 854×480 | 480×854 | 480×480 | 640×480 | 480×640 | 480×600 |
| 720p | 1280×720 | 720×1280 | 720×720 | 960×720 | 720×960 | 720×900 |
| 1080p | 1920×1080 | 1080×1920 | 1080×1080 | 1440×1080 | 1080×1440 | 1080×1350 |
| 4K | 3840×2160 | 2160×3840 | 2160×2160 | 2880×2160 | 2160×2880 | 2160×2700 |

**Lite and Starter support up to 1080p. Creator and Pro support up to 4K.** Lite also has an export count limit and watermark; see [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing).

## Frame rate

Choose **Auto**, **24**, **30**, or **60 fps**. Auto is the default and chooses from the supported frame rates based on your source footage. Increasing the export frame rate does not create new detail in low-frame-rate footage.

## Quality

Choose **Very Low, Low, Medium, High, or Very High**. Higher quality generally increases the file size. High is the default; MP4 at 1080p and High quality is a useful starting point for reviewing an edit.

Video-file settings are separate from [timeline exports to other editors](/export/export-your-video), which use their own interchange format.
