## Export a video file

1. Click **Export** at the top right of the editor, or press **Cmd/Ctrl+E** with the default shortcuts.
2. Choose **Video File** and select a format: MP4, WebM, MOV, or MKV.
3. Choose resolution, frame rate, and quality. See [Formats & Quality Settings](/export/formats-and-quality).
4. Start the export. Choose a save location if prompted; otherwise, save the downloaded file when rendering finishes.

Lite includes 3 lifetime exports with a watermark, up to 1080p. Starter has unlimited watermark-free exports up to 1080p. Creator and Pro have unlimited watermark-free exports up to 4K. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-and-pricing).

## Where rendering happens

- **Web:** Cardboard can render in your browser or in the cloud. For eligible MP4 exports, both run and the first completed result is used. Other projects may use only one path. WebM, MOV, and MKV exports normally use cloud rendering.
- **Mac app:** Videos render locally on your Mac.
- **Claude and ChatGPT connector:** Videos render in the cloud. Follow the [connector documentation](https://docs.cardboard.ai/mcp).

Export time depends on the project length, resolution, effects, device, and rendering path. Check the progress indicator; if an export fails, see [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting).

## Continue editing in another application

Timeline export is available in the **web editor on Starter, Creator, and Pro**. It is not available in the Mac app.

| Export tab | File | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Final Cut / DaVinci | FCPXML | Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve |
| Premiere Pro | XML | Adobe Premiere Pro |

Choose the relevant tab in the Export dialog. Use the option to include media if you want the source assets alongside the timeline. Import the timeline in the destination editor and relink media if prompted.

Timeline files are written at 30 fps. Review the imported edit before delivering it: effects, captions, and other editor-specific features may not transfer identically. Export a rendered video when you need the finished appearance.
