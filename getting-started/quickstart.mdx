<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload and Tell AI What You Want">
    Go to [usecardboard.com/app/home](https://usecardboard.com/app/home) and create a free account. 

    Drag your clips into Cardboard. Now here's the key - Cardboard is a listener. Don't hold back on details.

    ![Sign up and create a new project in Cardboard](/images/hero.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give Prompt">
    The prompt I’m using is:

    "Build the edit around @voiceover.m4a for an Instagram reel. Match visuals to the narration, choose the most relevant clips, and keep the rhythm tight and intentional"

    **What's @voiceover.m4a?** The @ lets you tag specific files. It's like pointing and saying "Use this one here."

    **Supported files:** MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, WAV, PNG, JPG, GIF
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Model and Aspect Ratio">
    **Before you hit send:**

    - **Bottom left of chatbox** – Pick your aspect ratio (6:11, 1:1, etc)
    - **Bottom right** – Choose your AI model (let's use Claude Sonnet 4.5)

      Hit Enter.

    ![Adding clips to the timeline in Cardboard](/images/hero.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let AI Cook">
    Cardboard's working. Grab coffee. Scroll some reels. This takes about 30-60 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your video">
    Your edit is ready. But the screen looks busy, right? Let me break it down:

    - **Left panel (Media Library) **– Your control centre for manual editing and media uploading
    - **Centre panel (Canvas)** – Your video preview. Hit **Play** to watch what AI made.
    - **Bottom panel (Timeline)** – All your clips laid out. Drag them to reorder. That's simple.
    - **Right panel (AI Chat)** – Keep the conversation going.

    Watch it through. See what AI did. Maybe it nailed it. Maybe you want changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export your video">
    Happy with it? Click **Export** in the top-right corner. Pick your settings. Done.

    **Want to tweak it?** You've got options:

    Keep iterating until it feels right. Then export.

    ![Export dialog in Cardboard](/images/hero.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example prompts to try next

Once you have the basics down, try these prompts in the AI Chat:

- "Add a title card that says 'My First Video' at the beginning"
- "Speed up the middle section to 2x"
- "Add a fade transition between every clip"
- "Make this video vertical for Instagram Reels"
- "Find the funniest moment and make it a 15-second clip"

<Tip>
  You don't have to do everything with AI. You can manually trim, split, and rearrange clips on the timeline at any time. Use whatever mix of AI and manual editing feels right.
</Tip>
