---
title: Your First Video in 5 Minutes
description: Go from zero to a finished video with AI-powered editing.
---

This guide walks you through creating your first video in Cardboard. You will sign up, upload media, give the AI an editing instruction, and export a finished video.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and open the editor">
    Go to [app.usecardboard.com](https://app.usecardboard.com) and create a free account. Once you are in, click **New Project** to open the editor.

    <img src="/images/hero.png" alt="Sign up and create a new project in Cardboard" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your media">
    Drag and drop video files, images, or audio into the **Media Library** panel on the left side of the editor. You can also click **Upload** to browse your files.

    Cardboard supports MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, WAV, PNG, JPG, and GIF files.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add clips to the timeline">
    Click on any media file in your library and drag it onto the **Timeline** at the bottom of the screen. You can add multiple clips — they will play in order from left to right.

    <img src="/images/hero.png" alt="Adding clips to the timeline in Cardboard" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give the AI an editing command">
    Open the **AI Chat** panel on the right side of the editor. Type a plain-English instruction and press **Enter**. Here are some commands to try:

    - "Trim this video to the most exciting 30 seconds"
    - "Add captions to the entire video"
    - "Create a fast-paced montage with transitions"
    - "Remove all the silent parts"
    - "Add upbeat background music"

    The AI will process your request and apply the edits to your timeline. You can see exactly what changed and undo anything you don't like.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview your video">
    Click the **Play** button on the canvas in the center of the editor to preview your video. If you want to make adjustments, type another instruction in the AI Chat or manually drag clips on the timeline.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export your video">
    When you are happy with the result, click the **Export** button in the top-right corner. Choose your resolution and format, then click **Export**. Your video will render and be ready to download in a few moments.

    <img src="/images/hero.png" alt="Export dialog in Cardboard" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You don't have to do everything with AI. You can manually trim, split, and rearrange clips on the timeline at any time. Use whatever mix of AI and manual editing feels right.
</Tip>

## Example prompts to try next

Once you have the basics down, try these prompts in the AI Chat:

- "Add a title card that says 'My First Video' at the beginning"
- "Speed up the middle section to 2x"
- "Add a fade transition between every clip"
- "Make this video vertical for Instagram Reels"
- "Find the funniest moment and make it a 15-second clip"
