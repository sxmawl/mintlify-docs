Cardboard helps you turn footage into a video by describing the edit you want. Review the first cut, refine it in chat or on the timeline, then export.

## Choose where to edit

- **Web editor:** Open [Cardboard](https://www.cardboard.ai/app/home) in Chrome on your computer. These guides cover the web editor.
- **Mac app:** Download [Cardboard for Apple Silicon Macs](https://www.cardboard.ai/desktop). It saves projects on your Mac and includes the `cb` command-line tool.
- **Claude and ChatGPT:** Follow the [connector documentation](https://docs.cardboard.ai/mcp) to edit videos in chat, including on your phone.

Projects in the web editor, Mac app, and connector are separate; they do not automatically appear in the other surfaces.

## Make your first video

1. Add your footage in the web editor.
2. Describe the video you want, including its length and aspect ratio.
3. Preview the edit and make changes in chat or on the timeline.
4. Export using the options available on your plan.
